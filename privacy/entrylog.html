Entrylog

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026 · Developer: Product Labs

The short version

  • Your permits, gas readings, fire watch photos, inspections and signatures stay on your phone. Product Labs has no servers for Entrylog and never receives them.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no location tracking.
  • A record leaves your phone only when you share a PDF or save an encrypted backup.
  • Google Play handles subscription payments. We never see your card details.

1. Who we are

Entrylog ("the app") helps safety leads and supervisors keep confined space entry permits, hot work permits with fire watch logs, and weekly hazardous waste inspection records. It is made by Product Labs ("we", "us"). Entrylog is not affiliated with or endorsed by OSHA, the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, NFPA or any state agency.

2. What Entrylog stores, and where

Everything you enter or capture is saved in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. This can include:

  • Your company profile and logo.
  • Confined spaces, their entry points and QR label numbers.
  • Entry permits: the names of entrants, attendants and entry supervisors, hazards, isolation steps, gas readings with tester initials and times, who went in and out and when, rescue service names and phone numbers you type, notes and signatures.
  • Hot work permits: the names of operators, the permit authorizer and the fire watch, checklist answers, fire watch check-ins with photos and notes, and signatures.
  • Hazardous waste accumulation areas: containers, waste descriptions and codes, start and shipping dates, manifest numbers, weekly inspection answers, photos and signatures.

We do not receive, sell or share any of this information. We cannot see it. Entrylog does not use your location.

Information about other people. Names, initials and phone numbers of co-workers, contractors or rescue services that you record belong to your business records and stay on your device. If someone asks about information you recorded about them, they should contact your business.

3. Permissions and why the app asks

Camera
To take photos for permits, fire watch check-ins and inspections, and to scan Entrylog's QR labels. Scanning happens on your device. Photos are saved in the app's private storage, not in your gallery.
Notifications
For fire watch check-in alerts and reminders before permits, inspections and container dates fall due. Alerts are created on your phone.
Alarms & reminders optional
Lets fire watch alerts arrive at the exact minute, even with the screen locked. Entrylog asks for it when you start your first fire watch. Without it, alerts can come late.
Run at startup
Lets Entrylog set a running fire watch's alerts again after your phone restarts.

You can refuse any of these or turn them off later in your phone's settings, and Entrylog keeps working. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, files or photo library. When you add a logo from your photos, Android's photo picker lets you choose one image to share with the app.

4. Sharing, exports and backups

  • Sharing a record. When you share an entry permit, a fire watch log, a weekly inspection log, an annual review pack or a CSV export, Android's share sheet sends it to the app or person you choose, such as email, a messaging app or cloud storage. That service's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Encrypted backups. An Entrylog backup file (.apexbak) is encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose (AES-GCM). You pick where to save it, for example Google Drive or a USB drive. We never receive the file or the passphrase, so we cannot recover a lost passphrase.
  • Android device backup. Entrylog keeps its records out of Android's automatic cloud backup. Use Entrylog's encrypted backup to move records to a new phone.

5. Subscriptions and Google Play Billing

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing. Google collects and processes your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy, and the billing library in the app exchanges purchase and technical information with Google Play to do its job. Entrylog never sees your card or bank details; the app only checks, on your device, whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it anytime in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. If your subscription ends, you can still open, finish and export your records.

6. No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Entrylog contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools from third parties, and it does not use your advertising ID. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your phone, Google may give us summary crash and performance statistics through Google Play Console. Those statistics do not include your records.

7. Internet use

Entrylog works fully offline, and the app never uploads your records. Google Play uses the internet for purchases and app updates. Links in the app, such as the link to this privacy policy, open in your own web browser.

8. Keeping and deleting your records

  • You can delete any record, space or area in the app. OSHA asks for cancelled confined space entry permits to be kept for at least one year, so Entrylog warns you before you delete one that is still inside that period. The app never deletes records on its own.
  • Uninstalling Entrylog deletes all app data from your device. Files you exported or backed up stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
  • Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

9. Children

Entrylog is a business tool for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Security

Android keeps each app's private storage separate from other apps. Protect your phone with a screen lock, and keep your backup passphrase somewhere safe. Backups are encrypted before they leave the app.

11. Your choices and rights

You control your data on your device: you can view, correct, export or delete it at any time in the app. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
