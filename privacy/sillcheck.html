SillCheck

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026 · Developer: Product Labs

The short version

  • Your job records, photos and signatures stay on your phone. Product Labs has no servers for SillCheck and never receives them.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • You decide when a record leaves your phone: when you share a PDF or save an encrypted backup.
  • Google Play handles subscription payments. We never see your card details.

1. Who we are

SillCheck ("the app") is a record-keeping app for renovation firms that follow the U.S. EPA Renovation, Repair and Painting rule. It is made by Product Labs ("we", "us"). SillCheck is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency.

2. What SillCheck stores, and where

Everything you enter or capture is saved in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. This can include:

  • Your firm's name, address, certification numbers and logo.
  • Your certified renovators' and workers' names, certificate numbers, course dates and certificate photos.
  • Job records: property address, owner and occupant names and contact details, pamphlet receipts, signatures, test results, work-practice photos, cleaning verification results and sign-offs.
  • Optional photo location tags, only if you switch on “Add location to photos” in Settings (one location per job, taken while the camera is open).

We do not receive, sell or share any of this information. We cannot see it.

Information about your customers. You may record details about property owners, tenants, parents or child-care staff because the RRP rule requires it. That information belongs to your firm's records and stays on your device. If one of your customers asks about their information, they should contact your firm.

3. Permissions and why the app asks

Camera
To take job photos (warning signs, containment, test swabs, cleaning checks). Photos are saved in the app's private storage, not in your gallery.
Location (optional)
Only if you turn on photo geotagging for a job. The app asks once per job, uses your location only while the app is open, and saves the coordinates with the photo on your device. The app never asks for background location.
Notifications
For reminders about renovator and firm certificates, owner-copy deadlines and record retention dates. Reminders are created on your phone.

You can refuse or later turn off any of these in your phone's settings. SillCheck keeps working; for example, photos are simply saved without a location. The app does not ask for access to your contacts, microphone, files or photo library. When you add a logo, Android's photo picker lets you choose one image to share with the app.

4. Sharing, exports and backups

  • Sharing a record. When you share a compliance record PDF or a CSV export, Android's share sheet sends it to the app or person you choose, such as email, a messaging app or cloud storage. That service's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Encrypted backups. A SillCheck backup file (.apexbak) is encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose (AES-GCM). You pick where to save it, for example Google Drive or a USB drive. We never receive the file or the passphrase, so we cannot recover a lost passphrase.
  • Android device backup. SillCheck keeps its records out of Android's automatic cloud backup. Use SillCheck's encrypted backup to move records to a new phone.

5. Subscriptions and Google Play Billing

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing. Google collects and processes your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy. SillCheck never sees your card or bank details. The app only checks with Google Play, on your device, whether your subscription is active. The Google Play Billing library that handles purchases may send technical diagnostics about the purchase process to Google; it contains none of your records. You can manage or cancel it anytime in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. If your subscription ends, you can still open and export your records.

6. No ads, no analytics, no tracking

SillCheck contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools from third parties, and it does not use your advertising ID. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your phone, Google may give us summary crash and performance statistics through Google Play Console. Those statistics do not include your records.

7. Internet use

SillCheck works fully offline. Your records are never uploaded by the app. Google Play uses the internet for purchases and app updates. Links to EPA or state program pages open in your own web browser.

8. Keeping and deleting your records

  • You can delete any job in the app. The RRP rule asks firms to keep records for 3 years after a job is completed, so SillCheck warns you before you delete a record that is still inside that period. The app never deletes records on its own.
  • Uninstalling SillCheck deletes all app data from your device. Files you exported or backed up stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
  • Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

9. Children

SillCheck is a business tool for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A firm may note facts such as "a child under 6 lives here" because the rule depends on them; that note stays on the firm's device.

10. Security

Android keeps each app's private storage separate from other apps. Protect your phone with a screen lock, and keep your backup passphrase somewhere safe. Backups are encrypted before they leave the app.

11. Your choices and rights

You control your data on your device: you can view, correct, export or delete it at any time in the app. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
