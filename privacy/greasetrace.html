GreaseTrace

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026 · Developer: Product Labs

The short version

  • Your customers, kitchens, job photos and signatures stay on your phone. Product Labs runs no servers for GreaseTrace and never receives them.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not use your location.
  • A record leaves your phone only when you share a PDF, a label sheet or a CSV file, or save an encrypted backup.
  • Google Play handles subscription payments. We never see your card details.

1. Who we are

GreaseTrace ("the app") is a record-keeping app for contractors who inspect and clean commercial kitchen exhaust systems. It is made by Product Labs ("we", "us"). GreaseTrace is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Fire Protection Association or any fire department.

2. What GreaseTrace stores, and where

Everything you enter or capture is saved in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. This can include:

  • Your company's name, address, phone, email, license or registration numbers and logo.
  • Your customers and their kitchens: business names, addresses, contact names, phone numbers, email addresses and access notes.
  • Exhaust system details: hoods, filters, ducts, fans, access panel locations, fire-suppression notes and QR label codes.
  • Job records: before and after photos, photos of areas that could not be cleaned, grease depth readings, deficiencies, technician names and certificate numbers, and the signatures of technicians and customers.

We do not receive, sell or share any of this information. We cannot see it.

Information about your customers. You may record names, phone numbers and signatures of the people you work with at each kitchen. That information belongs to your business records and stays on your device. If one of your customers asks about their information, they should contact your company.

3. Permissions and why the app asks

Camera
To take job photos and to scan the QR labels on exhaust systems. QR codes are read on your device. Photos are saved in the app's private storage, not in your gallery, and each one carries the date and time it was taken.
Notifications
For reminders when a system is due for service and when a written report still needs to be sent. Reminders are created on your phone.

You can refuse or later turn off either permission in your phone's settings. GreaseTrace keeps working; you can still type a label code instead of scanning it. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, files or photo library. When you add a logo, Android's photo picker lets you choose one image to share with the app.

4. Sharing, exports and backups

  • Sharing a record. When you share a certificate, a deficiency report, a label sheet or a CSV file, Android's share sheet sends it to the app or person you choose, such as email, a messaging app, a printer or cloud storage. That service's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Encrypted backups. A GreaseTrace backup file (.apexbak) is encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose (AES-GCM). You pick where to save it, for example Google Drive or a USB drive. We never receive the file or the passphrase, so we cannot recover a lost passphrase.
  • Android device backup. GreaseTrace keeps its records out of Android's automatic cloud backup. Use GreaseTrace's encrypted backup to move records to a new phone.

5. Subscriptions and Google Play Billing

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing, a service provided by Google. Google collects and processes your payment information, and any information its billing service needs, under the Google Privacy Policy. GreaseTrace never sees your card or bank details. The app only checks with Google Play, on your device, whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it anytime in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. If your subscription ends, you can still open and export your records.

6. No ads, no analytics, no tracking

GreaseTrace contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools from third parties, and it does not use your advertising ID. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your phone, Google may give us summary crash and performance statistics through Google Play Console. Those statistics do not include your records.

7. Internet use

GreaseTrace works fully offline, on rooftops and in basements alike. The app never uploads your records. Google Play uses the internet for purchases and app updates. Links to outside pages open in your own web browser.

8. Keeping and deleting your records

  • You can delete any customer, kitchen, system or job in the app. GreaseTrace never deletes records on its own, because kitchen owners, insurers and fire marshals may ask to see older service records.
  • Uninstalling GreaseTrace deletes all app data from your device. Files you exported or backed up stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
  • Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

9. Children

GreaseTrace is a business tool for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Security

Android keeps each app's private storage separate from other apps. Protect your phone with a screen lock, and keep your backup passphrase somewhere safe. Backups are encrypted before they leave the app.

11. Your choices and rights

You control your data on your device: you can view, correct, export or delete it at any time in the app. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
