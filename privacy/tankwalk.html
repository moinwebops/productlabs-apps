Tankwalk

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026 · Developer: Product Labs

The short version

  • Your sites, walkthroughs, photos and signatures stay on your phone. Product Labs has no servers for Tankwalk and never receives them.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no location tracking.
  • A record leaves your phone only when you share a PDF or save an encrypted backup.
  • Google Play handles subscription payments. We never see your card details.

1. Who we are

Tankwalk ("the app") helps owners and operators of underground storage tanks keep walkthrough inspection records. It is made by Product Labs ("we", "us"). Tankwalk is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency or any state agency.

2. What Tankwalk stores, and where

Everything you enter or capture is saved in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. This can include:

  • Your company profile and logo, and the name and role of each person who signs a walkthrough.
  • Sites and their equipment: addresses, facility IDs, owner and operator names, tanks, piping, spill buckets, sumps, release detection equipment and QR label numbers.
  • Walkthrough records: answers, notes on actions taken, equipment photos, photos of ATG printouts and the values you type from them, and signatures.
  • Delivery logs, contractor test results and photos of their reports.
  • Alarm and incident entries, including who you notified, when, and any case number.
  • Contact details you add for a site, such as your state UST agency, fire department or repair contractor.

We do not receive, sell or share any of this information. We cannot see it. Tankwalk does not use your location.

Information about other people. Names and phone numbers of agency staff, contractors or co-workers that you record belong to your business records and stay on your device. If someone asks about information you recorded about them, they should contact your business.

3. Permissions and why the app asks

Camera
To photograph equipment, ATG printouts and contractor reports, and to scan Tankwalk's QR labels. Scanning happens on your device. Photos are saved in the app's private storage, not in your gallery.
Notifications
For reminders before a walkthrough, yearly check, delivery or test falls due. Reminders are created on your phone.

You can refuse either permission or turn it off later in your phone's settings, and Tankwalk keeps working. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, files or photo library. When you add a logo or a report scan from your photos, Android's photo picker lets you choose one image to share with the app.

4. Sharing, exports and backups

  • Sharing a record. When you share a walkthrough PDF, a 12-month binder, an annual summary or a CSV export, Android's share sheet sends it to the app or person you choose, such as email, a messaging app or cloud storage. That service's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Encrypted backups. A Tankwalk backup file (.apexbak) is encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose (AES-GCM). You pick where to save it, for example Google Drive or a USB drive. We never receive the file or the passphrase, so we cannot recover a lost passphrase.
  • Android device backup. Tankwalk keeps its records out of Android's automatic cloud backup. Use Tankwalk's encrypted backup to move records to a new phone.

5. Subscriptions and Google Play Billing

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing. Google collects and processes your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy, and the billing library in the app exchanges purchase and technical information with Google Play to do its job. Tankwalk never sees your card or bank details; the app only checks, on your device, whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it anytime in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. If your subscription ends, you can still open and export your records.

6. No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Tankwalk contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools from third parties, and it does not use your advertising ID. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your phone, Google may give us summary crash and performance statistics through Google Play Console. Those statistics do not include your records.

7. Internet use

Tankwalk works fully offline, and the app never uploads your records. Google Play uses the internet for purchases and app updates. Links to EPA pages, such as the list of state UST program contacts, open in your own web browser.

8. Keeping and deleting your records

  • You can delete any record, or a whole site, in the app. Federal rules ask for walkthrough records to be kept for one year, and some test records for three years, so Tankwalk warns you before you delete a record that is still inside its keep period. The app never deletes records on its own.
  • Uninstalling Tankwalk deletes all app data from your device. Files you exported or backed up stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
  • Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

9. Children

Tankwalk is a business tool for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Security

Android keeps each app's private storage separate from other apps. Protect your phone with a screen lock, and keep your backup passphrase somewhere safe. Backups are encrypted before they leave the app.

11. Your choices and rights

You control your data on your device: you can view, correct, export or delete it at any time in the app. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
